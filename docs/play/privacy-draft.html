Lilyly privacy policy

What stays on your device

Lilyly lets you record cycle and fertility observations, mood, sleep, medication, therapy notes, safety plans, journal pages, readings and rituals. It uses these records to show the views, reminders and comparisons you choose. It has no Lilyly account or backend. This build does not request Internet access and contains no ads or analytics service.

Storage and access

Record text is encrypted with an Android Keystore key. Imported books, covers and copied images use app-private storage; they are not individually encrypted by Lilyly. Some selected files are read from the document provider you choose. Android handles device authentication. Lilyly does not receive your fingerprint. You can enable app lock or hide screenshots in Settings & privacy.

Permissions and choices

Notifications are optional and show generic medication reminders. Lilyly uses Android’s restart and time-change signals to reschedule them. The system file picker and share menu let you choose particular documents and images. Lilyly does not need your contacts, location, microphone or broad photo-library access.

Exports and links

When you select a destination for a backup or reading-copy PDF, that destination may be a cloud provider. Backups are password-encrypted; PDFs are readable by whoever receives the file. Opening a source link launches an external app, whose privacy practices apply. There is no automatic upload of journal or health records to Lilyly.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you remove them. Android Settings → Apps → Lilyly → Storage → Clear data removes the records and private files for that installation. Exported copies, original files, backups and other installations remain until you remove them separately. Lilyly cannot recover your backup password.
