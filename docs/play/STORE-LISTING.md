# Lilyly

Short description: A private journal, cycle garden and grimoire for the rhythms of your days.

Full description:

Open Lilyly to a daily page with the moon, your cycle and a place to check in. Build a notebook from words, photographs, drawings and small things you want to remember. A day in your calendar can lead back to the journal pages, sleep and moods you recorded there.

In the Cycle Garden, review recorded periods, tap a day to log symptoms and look back at past cycles. Add an optional waking temperature and see your measured readings over the last month. Sanctuary keeps check-ins, therapy notes and medication logs in one place. The Reading Nook holds books you import yourself; save passages or bring a quote into your journal.

Explore tarot as a reflective practice, keep a personal ritual record or begin a page with the estimated moon phase. Choose a theme, protect Lilyly with your device lock and export a password-protected backup.

Lilyly is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Period and fertility dates are estimates and must not be used as contraception. Ask a qualified healthcare professional about treatment, medicines or fertility decisions.

Release notes: Adds waking-temperature logging and a 30-day chart, an offline privacy notice, and a botanical launcher icon. An intentionally empty journal stays empty after reopening.
