LILYLY · GOOGLE PLAY TESTING KIT

1. To try Lilyly on your own Samsung, use the separate preview APK download.
2. To prepare a Play test, open PUBLISH.md. The production app-release.aab
   inside this kit must be signed with your own upload key first. It is not
   an APK and is not installed by tapping it on a phone.
3. Store artwork and synthetic screenshots are in artwork/. The proposed
   short and full descriptions are in STORE-LISTING.md.
4. Edit privacy-draft.html with your PUBLIC publisher name and a monitored
   privacy contact. Host it as a public webpage and check it on your phone.
5. Read STORE-QA.md before public release. No Play listing or production
   rollout has been created by preparing this kit.

Never send your upload key or passwords in chat.
