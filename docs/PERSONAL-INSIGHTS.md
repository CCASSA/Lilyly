# Threads over time — 0.15

Open **More → Patterns & insights**, or **Sanctuary → Patterns**. Choose insight data before any source is included. Sleep, Sanctuary, Cycle and Dreams are independent choices; the selected window is 30, 90 or 365 calendar days through today. Choices persist in encrypted settings and participate in the existing backup merge.

## Comparisons

- Rest with Sanctuary anxiety, mood and energy ratings.
- Rest with explicitly recorded Cycle pain ratings.
- Recurring dream symbols from the user's own tags.

Rest uses manually recorded time in bed, grouped by local waking date. Multiple valid, non-overlapping sleep records on a date are summed; overlapping records or totals above 24 hours exclude that date from rest comparisons. Invalid intervals, future dates and out-of-window dates are excluded. Time in bed is not represented as objectively measured time asleep.

Each rating is checked against its 0–10 scale. Only records marked as having explicit detailed ratings contribute. Multiple ratings on one date become one daily average, so repeated check-ins do not increase the number of matched dates.

Comparisons require at least five distinct matched dates in each of two groups: under six hours in bed, and at least seven hours in bed. Dates between six and seven hours are excluded from these groups. These are descriptive bins, not sleep targets or medical recommendations. The UI explicitly tells users not to change their sleep to make a comparison appear.

Every result displays group sizes, averages, and an expandable list of underlying dates and values. Tapping a date opens Threads of a day. Android Back and the visible back control return to insights. Associations are exploratory descriptions of selected self-reports, not causal findings, statistical significance, diagnoses, or medication advice.
